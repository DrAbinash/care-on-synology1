# TRAIN_SOP_001: New Employee Induction & Training Checklist
## Care Diagnostics ERP Standard Operating Procedure

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
*   **Purpose**: Ensure new clinical, financial, and administrative employees are trained on correct ERP operations to avoid data entry errors or billing leaks.
*   **Scope**: Staff onboarding and induction procedures.
*   **Responsibility**: HR Manager, Department Heads, and IT Training Lead.

---

## 2. Onboarding Program Outline

### Day 1: System Onboarding & Credentials
1.  Verify the employee has signed the HIPAA patient data confidentiality agreement.
2.  Request the IT Administrator to provision a personal ERP user account with matching RBAC permissions.
3.  Instruct the employee to set up a secure password and enroll their biometric fingerprint at the workstation terminal.

### Day 2: Role-Specific Simulation (Staging Sandbox)
1.  Log the employee into the staging/sandbox environment.
2.  Execute standard daily workflows under supervision:
    *   **Receptionists**: Search/register dummy patients and print queue slips.
    *   **Billing Clerks**: Create mock bills, apply discounts, and record payments.
    *   **Imaging Technicians**: Fetch mock patients from worklists and execute DICOM pushes.
    *   **Clinicians**: Dictate reports, review findings, and apply digital signatures.

### Day 3: Release to Production
1.  Allow the employee to operate on the live production environment.
2.  Pair them with a senior mentor for real-time supervision during the first week.

---

## 3. Training Verification Checklist
- [ ] Account provisioned with correct role group.
- [ ] Confirmed understanding of HIPAA patient confidentiality protocols.
- [ ] Completed mock registration, billing, or reporting drills.
