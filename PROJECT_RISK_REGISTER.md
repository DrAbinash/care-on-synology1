# Project Risk Register

**Status:** Companion to `AI_PLATFORM_IMPLEMENTATION_MASTER_ROADMAP.md`. Every risk below is sourced from a prior document; this register's contribution is consolidating them into one trackable list with likelihood, impact, mitigation, and owner, for ongoing risk management rather than narrative discussion.

**Scoring convention:** Likelihood and Impact each rated Low/Medium/High. Risk Level is the combination, judged the same way each source document originally judged it, not mechanically derived.

## Risk Review Cadence

This register should be reviewed at minimum at every Milestone go-live decision, and likelihood/impact ratings should be updated based on real evidence as it accumulates, not left as the planning-phase estimates above indefinitely. R11 is the clearest existing example of why this matters: its likelihood rating is informed by a risk that already materialized once, in an earlier document's original Laboratory-assessment error, and was caught only because a correction step happened. This register exists so future risks get caught the same way, proactively, rather than only in retrospect.
