PHASE 4 — SPECIFIC TEST FINDINGS
====================================================

CASE 1 — MRI BRAIN

Normal case:
Appropriate normal brain anatomy.

Abnormal case:
Mild chronic small-vessel ischemic changes
and generalized cerebral volume loss.

Verify correct Findings and Impression.

CASE 2 — MRI LS SPINE

Normal case:
Normal lumbar anatomy.

Abnormal case:
Loss of lumbar lordosis.
Multilevel disc desiccation.
L4-L5 diffuse disc bulge.
L5-S1 posterior disc protrusion.
Mild facet arthropathy.

Verify:

- Correct vertebral numbering
- L5 as lowest prominent lumbar vertebra
  when applicable to the test anatomy
- Level-specific findings
- Canal measurement table
- No inappropriate normal statements

Measurement values must come from explicit
synthetic test inputs, not invented measurements
of DICOM images.

CASE 3 — MRI CERVICAL SPINE

Normal case:
Normal cervical anatomy.

Abnormal case:
Straightening of cervical lordosis.
C4-C5 and C5-C6 disc osteophyte complexes.
Mild canal narrowing.

Verify:

- Cord description
- Disc-level anatomy
- Foraminal findings when supplied
- Cervical canal measurements
- Correct Impression

CASE 4 — MRI BRAIN WITH CERVICAL SCREENING

Normal case:
Normal brain with unremarkable limited
cervical screening.

Abnormal case:
Mild cerebral small-vessel changes.
Cervical screening showing multilevel
degenerative changes.

Verify that:

- MRI Brain remains the primary examination.
- Cervical screening is separately identified.
- Screening does not claim detailed findings
  beyond the acquired sequences.
- The approved screening limitation statement
  is included where required.
- The PDF title accurately reflects study scope.

CASE 5 — MRI LS SPINE WITH WHOLE SPINE SCREENING

Normal case:
Normal lumbar spine and screening findings.

Abnormal case:
Lumbar degenerative disc disease.
Cervical screening degenerative changes.
Thoracic screening without major abnormality.

Verify:

- Lumbar spine remains the primary study.
- Cervical and thoracic screening are clearly
  separated.
- No duplicate lumbar findings.
- No unsupported detailed screening claims.
- Findings and Impression agree.
- Correct report title survives PDF generation.
