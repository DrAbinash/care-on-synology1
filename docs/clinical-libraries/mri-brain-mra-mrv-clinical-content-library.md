## A. MRA & MRV Brain (Angiography / Venography)

### 1. MRA - Intracranial Arterial Circulation (Circle of Willis)

- **Normal:** Major intracranial arteries (Bilateral ICAs, MCAs, ACAs, PCAs, Basilar, and Vertebral arteries) demonstrate normal course, lumen caliber, and uniform high-velocity signal without focal stenosis, occlusion, or aneurysm.
- **Abnormal:** **Saccular aneurysmal outpouching originating from the anterior communicating artery (ACom) measuring [X] mm (neck) x [X] mm (width) x [X] mm (height), directed [anteriorly/inferiorly] without surrounding thrombus.**
- **Abnormal:** **Saccular aneurysm arising from the bifurcation of the [right/left] middle cerebral artery (MCA) measuring [X] x [X] mm.**
- **Abnormal:** **Severe (70-80%) focal lumen narrowing / stenosis involving the M1 segment of the [left/right] MCA with secondary reduction in distal branch perfusion.**
- **Abnormal:** **Abrupt cut-off / complete absence of flow signal in the [right/left] internal carotid artery terminus extending into the M1 and A1 segments, diagnostic of acute arterial occlusion.**
- **Abnormal:** **Progressive occlusion of the bilateral terminal ICAs with a dense network of lenticulostriate collateral vessels ("puff of smoke" appearance), diagnostic of Moyamoya disease.**
- **Abnormal:** **Complex vascular malformation (AVM) centered in the [left/right] parietal lobe with a prominent feeding artery from the [MCA], a central nidus measuring [X] cm, and early draining veins into the superior sagittal sinus.**

### 2. MRA - Extracranial Carotid & Vertebral Arteries

- **Normal:** Extracranial internal carotid and vertebral arteries demonstrate symmetric caliber and intact luminal flow voids without dissecting hematoma or high-grade stenosis.
- **Abnormal:** **Eccentric crescentic T1 hyperintense intramural hematoma surrounding a narrowed lumen in the cervical segment of the [left/right] ICA, diagnostic of acute carotid artery dissection.**
- **Abnormal:** **High-grade atherosclerotic stenosis (>80% NASCET) at the [left/right] carotid bulb / proximal ICA with irregular lumen ulceration.**
- **Abnormal:** **Hypoplasia of the [right/left] vertebral artery terminating in PICA, representing a normal anatomic variant.**

### 3. MRV - Intracranial Dural Venous Sinuses & Deep Veins

- **Normal:** Dural venous sinuses (Superior Sagittal, Straight, Bilateral Transverse, and Sigmoid sinuses) and deep cerebral veins (Internal Cerebral Veins, Vein of Galen) demonstrate normal venous flow signal without filling defect.
- **Abnormal:** **Complete absence of normal venous flow signal replaced by T1 hyperintense / T2 hypointense clot filling the superior sagittal sinus, extending into the [left/right] transverse and sigmoid sinuses, diagnostic of acute dural venous sinus thrombosis.**
- **Abnormal:** **Focal filling defect within the [left/right] transverse sinus with peripheral rim enhancement on post-contrast MRV, consistent with subacute dural venous sinus thrombosis.**
- **Abnormal:** **Thrombosis of the deep venous system (internal cerebral veins and vein of Galen) with secondary bilateral thalamic edema on FLAIR.**
- **Abnormal:** **Congenital hypoplasia / aplasia of the [left/right] transverse sinus, a non-pathologic developmental variant.**

### Macro 2: Acute Dural Venous Sinus Thrombosis (Brain MRV)

FINDINGS:
Venous Sinuses:
- **Superior Sagittal Sinus:** **Complete loss of normal flow void, replaced by T1 hyperintense and T2/FLAIR iso-to-hypointense thrombus spanning from the anterior third to the torcular herophili.**
- **Transverse & Sigmoid Sinuses:** **Extension of non-occlusive thrombus into the proximal right transverse sinus. Left transverse and sigmoid sinuses demonstrate patent flow.**
- **Deep Veins:** **Internal cerebral veins, Vein of Galen, and Straight sinus are patent with preserved flow voids.**
Brain Parenchyma: **Focal area of cortical T2/FLAIR hyperintensity with cortical petechial blooming on SWI in the high parasagittal superior frontal gyrus, compatible with venous hemorrhagic infarction.**

IMPRESSION:
1. **Acute extensive dural venous sinus thrombosis involving the entire superior sagittal sinus and proximal right transverse sinus.**
2. **Secondary acute venous hemorrhagic infarction in the superior frontal cortex.**
