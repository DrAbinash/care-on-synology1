# X-Ray Cervical Spine Clinical Content Library

## 1. Views / Adequacy
Support AP, lateral, open-mouth odontoid, right/left oblique, flexion, extension, swimmer's and other/manual views.
Document inadequate visualization of C7-T1, odontoid, lateral masses or other required anatomy when applicable.

## 2. Normal Cervical Spine
FINDINGS:
Cervical alignment and lordosis are maintained. Vertebral body heights and intervertebral disc spaces are preserved. No acute fracture or listhesis is identified. Prevertebral soft tissues are not widened. Visualized posterior elements are intact. Odontoid and C1-C2 alignment are unremarkable when adequately visualized.

IMPRESSION:
No acute osseous abnormality identified on the provided cervical spine radiographs.

## 3. Alignment
- Preserved cervical lordosis.
- Straightening/loss of normal cervical lordosis.
- Reversal of cervical lordosis.
- Cervical scoliosis.
- Anterolisthesis / retrolisthesis of [vertebra] over [vertebra].
- Abnormality of anterior vertebral / posterior vertebral / spinolaminar / posterior spinous alignment line.

Straightening alone is nonspecific and should not automatically be attributed to muscle spasm.

## 4. Vertebral Bodies / Endplates
- Vertebral body heights maintained.
- Compression/wedging at [level].
- Cortical step/disruption.
- Marginal osteophytes.
- Endplate sclerosis/irregularity.
- Focal lucent/sclerotic/destructive osseous lesion.

## 5. Disc Spaces
Levels C2-3 through C7-T1 when visualized:
- preserved
- mild/moderate/severe disc-space reduction
- endplate osteophytes
- endplate sclerosis
- vacuum phenomenon if visible

## 6. Facets / Uncovertebral Joints / Foramina
- Facet arthropathy/hypertrophy.
- Uncovertebral hypertrophy.
- Neural foraminal narrowing on adequately positioned oblique view, with side and level.
Do not report foraminal narrowing from oblique views that were not performed or are nondiagnostic.

## 7. Craniocervical / C1-C2
- Odontoid intact when visualized.
- C1 lateral masses aligned with C2.
- Atlantoaxial alignment maintained.
- Odontoid fracture/suspicion.
- C1 lateral-mass asymmetry, with technical rotation considered.
- Atlanto-dental interval measurement when deliberately measured on an adequate view.

## 8. Prevertebral / Paraspinal Soft Tissues
- No prevertebral soft-tissue widening.
- Prevertebral soft-tissue swelling at [level].
- Soft-tissue calcification / other visible abnormality.

Do not use a single rigid numeric threshold across all ages/levels without appropriate context.

## 9. Degenerative Cervical Spondylosis
- Multilevel marginal osteophytes.
- Disc-space reduction at [levels].
- Endplate sclerosis.
- Uncovertebral/facet arthropathy.
- Foraminal narrowing on oblique views.
- Degenerative listhesis.

Suggested impression:
Multilevel cervical spondylotic changes, most pronounced at [level(s)].

## 10. Trauma
Support:
- fracture level/type/part
- displacement
- vertebral height loss
- facet malalignment/dislocation
- spinous-process fracture
- odontoid injury
- C1 injury pattern
- prevertebral swelling
- abnormal alignment

A technically inadequate radiographic series cannot exclude cervical injury. Allow radiologist-selected CT recommendation.

## 11. Dynamic Flexion / Extension
Only when actually performed:
- flexion translation
- extension translation
- angular change
- level
- range-of-motion limitation
- radiologist-selected dynamic instability

Do not infer instability from routine neutral radiographs.

## 12. Congenital / Developmental
- Block vertebra.
- Congenital fusion.
- Cervical rib.
- Transitional anatomy.
- Other segmentation anomaly.

## 13. Infection / Destructive Pattern
- Disc-space narrowing with adjacent endplate irregularity/destruction.
- Vertebral body destruction/collapse.
- Prevertebral soft-tissue swelling.
- Other focal destructive change.

Radiography may raise suspicion; etiology and activity require appropriate correlation.

## 14. Tumor / Osseous Lesion
- Focal lytic lesion.
- Focal sclerotic lesion.
- Mixed lesion.
- Vertebral collapse/pathologic fracture pattern.
Do not automatically diagnose metastasis from an indeterminate osseous abnormality.

## 15. Post-operative
- ACDF at [levels].
- Posterior fixation at [levels].
- Interbody cage/spacer.
- Plate/screws/rods.
- Alignment.
- Gross hardware continuity.
- Migration/breakage.
- Perihardware lucency when convincingly visible.
- Adjacent-segment degeneration.

Do not overstate loosening from equivocal radiographic lucency.

## 16. Impression Seeds
- No acute osseous abnormality on provided views.
- Straightening/loss of cervical lordosis.
- Multilevel cervical spondylosis, most pronounced at [level].
- [Grade/measurement if entered] anterolisthesis/retrolisthesis at [level].
- Acute fracture at [level] with [displacement/alignment effect].
- Dynamic translation at [level] on flexion/extension views; interpretation selected by radiologist.
- Post-operative changes as described.
