# USG & Doppler Roadmap

## Completed
* **Voluson Push (Phase 1)**: GE Voluson E9 network intake and image capture.
* **Direct DICOM Extraction Hierarchy (Phase 2)**: Direct retrieval of pixel data and headers.
* **PCPNDT Integration (Phase 3)**: Auto-population of Form F details from biometric measurements.
* **Measurement Provenance (Phase 5)**: Tracking origin (OCR vs DICOM SR vs Manual) of values.
* **AI Key Image Selection (Phase 6)**: Multi-candidate ranking and Sonologist approval workflow.
* **Pregnancy Timeline (Phase 7)**: Stepper representation of serial scans and Recharts progression plotting.
* **AI Sonologist Assistant (Phase 8)**: Side panels supporting quality review alerts (PASS/WARNING/CRITICAL) and post-dictation processing.
